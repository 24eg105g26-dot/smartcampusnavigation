Welcome to UniNav Campus System

How to Navigate:

  1. Search or Click: Use the global search bar or click directly on any campus building or entrance.
  2. Select Route: Pick your start location and destination in the Directions tab.
  3. Accessible Routing: Toggle Accessible Mode to automatically navigate through wheelchair ramps and avoid stairs.
  4. Virtual Walk: Click Start Walk to simulate step-by-step navigation with animated path progression.
  5. Indoor Navigation: Explore multi-floor building plans with room-by-room listings in the Indoor Directory.
